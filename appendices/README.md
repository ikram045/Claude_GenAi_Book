# Appendices

Optional deep dives. **None of them is a prerequisite for anything.**

Read one when a specific curiosity demands it — that's when mathematics sticks. Reading them out
of duty is Chapter 1's Mathematics Trap, and it's the most common way people quit this field.

| | Title | Pairs with | Read it when |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| **A** | [The Mathematics of Attention](A-attention-maths.md) | Chapter 5 | You want to know what attention *computes*, not just what it does |
| **B** | [Vector Mathematics](B-vector-maths.md) | Chapter 10 | You want to know why cosine similarity works, and why high-dimensional space is strange |
| **C** | [How Fine-Tuning Actually Works](C-finetuning-maths.md) | Chapter 27 | You want to know what "low-rank" means and why LoRA is so small |

## What you need to know first

Almost nothing:

- A vector is a list of numbers.
- A matrix is a grid of numbers.
- Multiplication and addition.

Everything else is explained where it appears. **Every symbol is defined the first time it's
used** — no notation is assumed.
